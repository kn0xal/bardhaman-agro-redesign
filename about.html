Who we are

From a small mill to a global grain partner.

Bardhaman Agro Products (I) Pvt. Ltd. began as a partnership firm in 2001. In 2009, we became a private limited company — with the same ambition we started with: bring dependable, premium rice to every home and every market we serve.

Today, our work spans sourcing, milling, quality assurance, packing and exports. We serve customers across India and in the UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar.
